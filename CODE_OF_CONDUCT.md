# Code of Conduct

Speak is built in the open with learners, teachers, translators and developers from many communities and languages. Many of the people Speak serves are children in classrooms. This code applies everywhere the project lives: the repository, issues, pull requests, chats, events and classroom pilots.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to people of every language, country, background, ability and level of experience. Nobody needs to write English well to take part.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you correct. Explain things the way you would to a curious learner.
- Respect every language Speak supports. Translations are decided with native speakers, not over them.
- Give credit for work, including translations, lessons and testing, not only code.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or mocking anyone's language, accent, spelling, culture or background.
- Sexual content or attention of any kind.
- Contacting a minor privately, or sharing photos, names or details of students without written permission from their school and guardians.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to a maintainer of the [speak-lang](https://github.com/speak-lang) organization. Reports are kept confidential. Anything involving a child's safety is passed to the school and, where required, to the relevant authorities.

## What happens next

Maintainers review every report and may respond with a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who break this code are held to the same standard.
